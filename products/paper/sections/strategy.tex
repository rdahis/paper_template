\section{Empirical Strategy}\label{sec:strategy}
\dependson{code/analysis/tab_example.R}

We estimate
\begin{equation}
  y_i = \alpha + \beta\, \text{Treated}_i + \gamma x_i + \varepsilon_i ,
\end{equation}
where $\beta$ is the parameter of interest. Identification relies on treatment
being as good as random conditional on $x_i$.
